## Why measure

You can’t improve what you can’t see—but vanity metrics create fear and gaming.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Prefer cycle time, throughput, escaped defects, and outcome KPIs over story-point velocity comparisons across teams. Use metrics to start conversations, not punish.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Comparing velocity across teams**.
- **Ignoring quality while chasing speed**.
- **No leading indicators for burnout/WIP**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Which three metrics would you show a CEO for a platform team?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Track cycle time for two weeks.
- [ ] Plot WIP aging once.
- [ ] Pair a delivery metric with a customer outcome.
