## Why awareness—not gatekeeping

Certifications can structure learning and signal shared vocabulary. They do not replace delivery judgment, facilitation skill, or domain context—especially in Bangladesh’s varied product/agency environments.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  PSM (Scrum.org) focuses on Scrum understanding. PMP (PMI) covers broader predictive/hybrid project management knowledge. Choose based on your context; never claim a cert is universally required.
</Callout>

## Practical advice

- Learn by running real ceremonies and shipping.
- Use cert study to fill gaps, not as a trophy hunt.
- On resumes, pair certs with outcomes you owned.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating certs as mandatory for junior roles**.
- **Memorizing guides without facilitation practice**.
- **Ignoring employer-specific delivery models**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Would you hire a PSM-certified PM with weak stakeholder skills? Why?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Skim official PSM/PMP pages and note audiences.
- [ ] List three skills a cert won’t prove.
- [ ] Pick one learning goal unrelated to exams.
