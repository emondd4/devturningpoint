## Why PMs own debt conversations

Debt is a product risk: slower delivery, more incidents, harder hiring. Ignoring it is a scope decision.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Catalog debt with impact (speed, reliability, security). Budget capacity (e.g., percentage of sprint) or theme sprints. Tie repayment to upcoming features when possible.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Only filing debt when something breaks**.
- **Endless refactors without outcomes**.
- **Punishing teams for past trade-offs**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How do you convince a CEO to spend a sprint on debt?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] List top five debt items with impact.
- [ ] Reserve capacity in the next sprint.
- [ ] Link one debt item to a roadmap feature.
