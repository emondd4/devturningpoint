## Why map dependencies

Critical path is often cross-team: APIs, designs, legal, app store review, payment partners.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Make dependencies visible early. Prefer small contracts and stubs over waiting for perfect integrations. Escalate blockers with options, not just status.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Discovering vendor lead times late**.
- **Hard-sequencing work that could be parallel**.
- **No owner for external follow-ups**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Design and API are both late. How do you keep a sprint productive?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] List dependencies for your next release.
- [ ] Assign an owner per external blocker.
- [ ] Propose one stub/mock strategy.
