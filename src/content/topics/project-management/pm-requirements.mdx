## Why requirements skill matters

Ambiguous needs create thrash. Good elicitation surfaces goals, constraints, and unknowns early.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Separate problem statements from solution prescriptions. Capture acceptance signals, non-functionals (security, performance), and open questions explicitly.
</Callout>

## Techniques

- Stakeholder interviews and job stories.
- Example mapping / concrete scenarios.
- Distinguish must / should / could with rationale.

## Common mistakes

- **Jumping to UI specs too early**.
- **Undocumented assumptions**.
- **Treating a BRD as unchangeable scripture**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How do you handle a stakeholder who only speaks in feature lists?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Interview one stakeholder with five clarifying questions.
- [ ] Write problem vs solution for a feature.
- [ ] List non-functional needs for a release.
