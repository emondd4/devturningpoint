## Why scope control

Uncontrolled scope destroys dates and morale. Cutting scope is a skill, not failure.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Tie scope to outcomes. Maintain an explicit in/out list. Route changes through backlog prioritization with impact on date/quality/cost.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Silent scope creep via chat**.
- **Padding instead of negotiating**.
- **Confusing MVP with low quality**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Market window is 4 weeks; estimate is 10. How do you cut scope without hiding risk?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write an in/out list for a launch.
- [ ] Facilitate one scope trade-off meeting.
- [ ] Document deferred items and risks.
