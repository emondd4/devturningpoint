## Why ceremonies exist

Scrum events create inspect-and-adapt loops. Without adaptation, meetings become status theater.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Planning selects a forecast toward a Sprint Goal. Daily is a 15-minute plan for the next 24 hours. Review inspects the Increment with stakeholders. Retro improves the system of work.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Daily as management reporting**.
- **Review without a working Increment**.
- **Retro with no experiments**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How do you rescue a daily standup that regularly runs 40 minutes?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write a Sprint Goal for a real sprint.
- [ ] Facilitate one retro with a single experiment.
- [ ] Timebox planning with a ready backlog.
