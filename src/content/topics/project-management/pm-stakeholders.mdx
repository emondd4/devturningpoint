## Why stakeholders

Projects fail socially as often as technically.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Map who decides, who uses, who blocks, who funds. Tailor communication. Never surprise sponsors late.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **One update for every audience**.
- **Avoiding conflict until explosion**.
- **Loudest voice as priority**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  A founder refuses scope cuts. How do you respond?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Draw a power/interest map.
- [ ] Draft weekly status with risks first.
- [ ] Practice a trade-off options slide.
