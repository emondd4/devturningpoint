## Why stories + AC

A backlog item without acceptance criteria invites mismatched expectations between product, design, eng, and QA.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Stories describe user value; AC define done examples. Prefer Given/When/Then or checklist bullets that QA can execute. Keep stories thin enough to finish in a sprint.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Tasks disguised as stories** (“create table”).
- **Vague AC** (“works well”).
- **Giant epics pulled into sprints unchanged**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Write AC for “user can reset password” including failure cases.
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Split one epic into three stories.
- [ ] Add negative AC to a story.
- [ ] Review a story with QA before sprint planning.
