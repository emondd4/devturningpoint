## Why Kanban

Interrupt-driven or ops-heavy teams often need flow optimization more than sprint commitments.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Visualize work, limit WIP, manage flow, make policies explicit, improve collaboratively. Use cycle time and aging WIP to spot bottlenecks.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Board without WIP limits** — Multitasking soup.
- **Columns that don’t match reality**.
- **No service-level expectations**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  When would you choose Kanban over Scrum for a product team?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Add WIP limits to a board.
- [ ] Measure cycle time for five items.
- [ ] Write explicit pull policies.
