## Why heroics do not scale

The first five minutes decide customer impact. Without roles, severity, and comms channels, everyone debugs in parallel and nobody updates stakeholders.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Detect → declare severity → mitigate user impact first → investigate → resolve → blameless postmortem with action items. Separate incident commander from deep debugger when the team is large enough.
</Callout>

```bash
# Mitigation-first checklist sketch
# 1) Confirm blast radius (error rate / region)
# 2) Rollback or feature-flag off
# 3) Scale healthy capacity if needed
# 4) Preserve evidence (logs, deploy id)
```

## Common mistakes

- **Debugging forever before rollback** — Restore service, then root-cause.
- **No single comms owner** — Duplicate/conflicting status updates.
- **Punitive postmortems** — Hides systemic causes.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Latency spiked after a deploy. What are your first 10 minutes?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write a one-page severity rubric.
- [ ] Practice a rollback runbook for your app.
- [ ] Draft a blameless postmortem template.
