## Why engineers own cost signals

Cloud bills are architecture feedback. Idle load balancers, oversized nodes, chatty cross-AZ traffic, and forgotten snapshots silently tax the team.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Cost ≈ rate × usage × waste. You control usage (scale policies) and waste (turn things off, rightsize, lifecycle data). Tags make spend attributable to a service or team.
</Callout>

## Practical levers

- Tag `service`, `env`, `owner` on every resource.
- Prefer autoscaling and schedules for non-prod.
- Watch data transfer and NAT gateway surprises.
- Delete orphan volumes, old AMIs, unused elastic IPs.

## Common mistakes

- **Optimizing before measuring** — Guessing saves nothing.
- **Non-prod always-on** — Often half the bill.
- **No owner tags** — Orphans live forever.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Your AWS bill doubled overnight. How do you investigate in the first hour?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] List top 5 cost drivers for a sample account conceptually.
