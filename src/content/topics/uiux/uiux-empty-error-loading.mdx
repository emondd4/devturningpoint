## Why states matter

Real users hit zero results, slow networks, and failures—those screens teach trust.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  For every view design loading, empty (first-use vs no-results), partial, error (retryable vs fatal), and success. Match copy to next action.
</Callout>

## Patterns

- Skeletons for predictable layout; spinners for unknown duration.
- Empty states with a single clear CTA.
- Errors with cause + recovery.

## Common mistakes

- **Blank white screens**.
- **Technical error codes only**.
- **Infinite spinners**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Design empty and error states for a failed payment confirmation.
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Audit three screens for missing states.
- [ ] Write recovery copy for one network error.
- [ ] Spec loading vs empty for search.
