## Why type is UX

Users scan first. Weak typography increases cognitive load even when colors look polished.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Establish a limited type scale (display, title, body, label, caption). Control line length, line-height, and weight for hierarchy—not random sizes.
</Callout>

## Practical rules

- Comfortable body line length (~45–75 characters).
- Clear contrast between heading and body roles.
- Prefer fonts with good Bangla/Latin coverage when needed.

## Common mistakes

- **Too many font sizes** — Visual noise.
- **Tight line-height on long text** — Fatigue.
- **All-caps body text** — Harder to read.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How would you define a type scale for a learning product with Bangla and English UI?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Document a 5-step type scale in Figma.
