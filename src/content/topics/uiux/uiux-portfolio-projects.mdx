## Why process stories win

Hiring managers hire judgment. Case studies beat moodboards.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  For each project: context, role, insights, decisions, trade-offs, final UI, and what you’d improve. Be honest about ownership.
</Callout>

## Project ideas

- Redesign a local service flow.
- Accessibility remediation case study.
- Design system starter (tokens + components).

## Common mistakes

- **Only Dribbble-like shots**.
- **Claiming unmeasurable impact**.
- **Ignoring constraints**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Walk through a portfolio piece where research changed direction.
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write a one-page case study outline.
- [ ] Add before/after and rationale.
- [ ] Prepare a 5-minute walkthrough.
