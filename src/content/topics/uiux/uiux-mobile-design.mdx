## Why mobile is different

Thumbs, interruptions, variable networks, and OS conventions change interaction design.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Design for one-handed reach, large targets, and progressive disclosure. Prioritize primary tasks; push secondary actions into menus.
</Callout>

## Patterns

- Bottom navigation for top tasks.
- Safe areas and keyboard avoidance.
- Offline/poor-network messaging.

## Common mistakes

- **Desktop hover assumptions**.
- **Tiny tap targets**.
- **Ignoring platform norms**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Redesign a dense desktop table workflow for a phone screen.
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Check tap target sizes.
- [ ] Prototype keyboard covering a CTA.
- [ ] Note iOS vs Android pattern differences.
