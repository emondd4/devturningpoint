## Why IA

If users can’t find it, the feature might as well not exist.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Group by user mental models—not org charts. Label plainly. Provide multiple findability paths.
</Callout>

## Techniques

- Card sorting and tree testing.
- Inventory content before redesign.

## Common mistakes

- **Company jargon in nav**.
- **Mega-menus as dumping grounds**.
- **Copying competitor IA blindly**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How would you restructure nav for tracks, topics, and careers?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Inventory top 20 destinations.
- [ ] Propose ≤7 top-level items.
- [ ] Tree-test with three people.
