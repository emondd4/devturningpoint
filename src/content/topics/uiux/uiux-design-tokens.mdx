## Why tokens

Hard-coded hex and magic spacing diverge across Figma and CSS. Tokens are the contract.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Primitive tokens → semantic tokens → component usage. Themes swap semantic mappings without rewriting screens.
</Callout>

## Handoff tip

Name tokens for meaning (`text.muted`), not appearance (`gray-500`).

## Common mistakes

- **Only documenting colors**.
- **One-off overrides everywhere**.
- **Tokens without ownership**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How would you introduce tokens to a team shipping without a design system?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] List 10 semantic tokens.
- [ ] Map them to CSS variables.
- [ ] Align Figma variables with eng names.
