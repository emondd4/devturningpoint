## Why research

Opinions aren’t evidence. Lightweight research reduces building the wrong thing.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Match method to question: interviews for why, usability tests for can they, surveys for breadth. State sample limitations.
</Callout>

## Lightweight toolkit

- 5–8 usability sessions.
- Problem interviews before solution interviews.
- Support-ticket mining.

## Common mistakes

- **Leading questions**.
- **One CEO interview as research**.
- **No synthesis**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  You have one week and no budget. How do you validate onboarding?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write a research question and pick a method.
- [ ] Draft a 20-minute usability script.
- [ ] Turn three findings into changes.
