## Why wireframe

Keep feedback on hierarchy and flow before color debates.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Grayscale boxes, realistic content lengths, annotated interactions. Promote to hi-fi after critique.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Hi-fi too early**.
- **Ignoring mobile**.
- **No annotations**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  When do you skip wireframes?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Wireframe a settings page.
- [ ] Annotate validation errors.
- [ ] Critique with a developer.
