## Why handoff fails

Pretty mocks without states force engineers to invent behavior.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Handoff is conversation plus artifacts: flows, tokens, responsive rules, empty/error/loading, a11y notes, and acceptance criteria.
</Callout>

## Checklist

- Prototypes for complex interactions.
- Explicit breakpoints.
- Open questions logged, not buried in chat.

## Common mistakes

- **Happy-path frames only**.
- **Conflicting mocks**.
- **No involvement after ticket starts**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  What do you include in a handoff package for a new settings section?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Write an a11y note for a custom control.
- [ ] Pair with eng for 30 minutes.
- [ ] Verify built UI against tokens.
