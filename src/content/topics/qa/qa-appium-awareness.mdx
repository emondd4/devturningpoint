## Why know Appium

Many Bangladesh products ship Android/iOS clients. Appium is a common cross-platform automation layer over WebDriver protocols—even if your team uses Maestro, Detox, or Flutter Driver instead.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Appium drives real or virtual devices via a server that speaks WebDriver. Locators and waits differ from browser E2E; device farms and OS versions multiply cost. Prefer API/unit layers for most coverage; automate critical mobile journeys only.
</Callout>

## Awareness checklist

- Native vs hybrid vs webview contexts.
- Emulator vs real device trade-offs.
- Gesture and permission challenges.
- How Flutter/React Native tooling may replace or complement Appium.

## Common mistakes

- **Porting web E2E 1:1 to mobile** — Too slow and brittle.
- **Ignoring device matrix** — One Pixel ≠ all Androids.
- **Skipping manual exploratory on devices** — Sensors and OS dialogs matter.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  When would you choose Appium versus Playwright for a product with web + Android apps?
</Callout>
