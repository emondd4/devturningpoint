## Why flakes destroy trust

Random red CI trains teams to ignore failures.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Artifacts → classify (race, shared state, env drift) → fix root cause before retries. Quarantine is temporary debt.
</Callout>

## Fixes

Prefer auto-waiting assertions; isolate data; compare local vs CI environments.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Suite fails 20% on CI, passes locally—debug plan?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Traces on first retry
- [ ] Remove one hard sleep
- [ ] Define a flake triage ritual
