## Why gates

Tests that are optional get skipped under pressure. Branch protection and required checks turn quality into a contract.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Layer gates: lint/unit on every PR (minutes) → integration/API on merge → critical E2E on main/release. Fail loud on high-risk regressions; keep flake rate visible as its own metric.
</Callout>

## Design tips

- Required status checks on protected branches.
- Shard long E2E; fail fast on smoke.
- Publish artifacts (reports, traces) for debugging.
- Separate “informational” jobs from blockers.

## Common mistakes

- **All E2E on every commit** — Slow feedback kills adoption.
- **No ownership of red builds** — Culture failure.
- **Skipping gates for “hotfixes” without audit** — Recurring outages.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  Design CI gates for a Nest + Next monorepo with a 15-minute PR budget.
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] List required vs optional checks on your main branch.
- [ ] Add a smoke Playwright job for critical paths.
- [ ] Define flake SLO (e.g., &lt;2% retries).
