## Why risk-based

You cannot exhaustively test. Rank areas by probability of failure and business/user damage.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Score features: likelihood (complexity, change volume, past defects) × impact (money, safety, trust, compliance). Spend deep testing on high scores; sample low scores.
</Callout>

## Inputs

- Change risk (diff size, new integrations).
- Historical bug hotspots.
- Security/privacy surfaces (auth, payments, PII).
- Regulatory and contractual obligations.

## Common mistakes

- **Equal depth everywhere** — Spreads thin.
- **Ignoring operational risk** — Monitoring, rollback, and data migration.
- **Static risk matrix** — Reassess each release.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  You have four hours before a release. How do you choose what to test?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Build a risk matrix for your product’s top 10 features.
- [ ] Map CI suites to high-risk journeys.
- [ ] Revisit scores after a production incident.
