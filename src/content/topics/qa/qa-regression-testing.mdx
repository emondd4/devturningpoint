## Why regression exists

Every fix can break something else. Regression testing asks: **did we keep the promises we already made to users?**

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Maintain a living suite: smoke (fast gate), core regression (business critical), and deep packs triggered by risk/area. Prefer automation for stable paths; explore novel risk manually.
</Callout>

## Practical approach

1. Tag cases by area and criticality.
2. On each PR: smoke + affected area packs.
3. Before release: broader regression + exploratory charter on changed surfaces.

## Common mistakes

- **Rerunning everything always** — Slow and ignored.
- **Never pruning** — Dead tests hide real signal.
- **No owner for failures** — Red builds rot.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  A one-line CSS change shipped. What regression scope do you run and why?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Define a 10-case smoke pack for your app.
- [ ] Tag 20 cases by product area.
- [ ] Write exit criteria for “regression done”.
