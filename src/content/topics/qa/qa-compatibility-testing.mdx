## Why matrices explode

Supporting “every browser ever” is impossible. Compatibility testing picks **where users and revenue concentrate**, then samples the long tail.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Build a small primary matrix (e.g. latest Chrome/Safari/Firefox + one old iOS/Android WebView if relevant). Automate smoke there; explore visual/layout risks on real devices for high-traffic flows.
</Callout>

## Practical approach

- Analytics → top browsers/devices.
- Playwright projects for primary browsers in CI.
- Device lab / cloud for touch and Safari quirks before release.

## Common mistakes

- **Only testing your laptop Chrome** — Misses Safari flex/gap bugs.
- **Huge unused device farms** — Cost without signal.
- **Ignoring WebView** — Mobile apps embedding checkout.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How would you justify a three-browser CI matrix to a stakeholder who wants twenty?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Pull top 3 browsers from analytics (or assume BD mobile Chrome/Safari).
- [ ] Configure one multi-project Playwright smoke.
- [ ] Note two known engine differences to watch.
