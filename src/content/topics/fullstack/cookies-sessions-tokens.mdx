## Why this choice dominates architecture

Auth storage decides CSRF needs, CORS credentials mode, mobile client design, and XSS blast radius.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Browser apps often use httpOnly Secure cookies for session IDs or tokens. SPAs/mobile may use bearer tokens in memory. SameSite and Secure flags matter; localStorage is XSS-sensitive.
</Callout>

## Practical guidance

- Prefer httpOnly cookies for web session tokens when possible.
- Understand SameSite=Lax/Strict/None (+ Secure).
- Rotate/revoke server-side session state when using opaque session IDs.

## Common mistakes

- **JWT in localStorage by default** — XSS steals sessions.
- **Missing Secure/SameSite in production**.
- **Assuming cookies work cross-site without CORS credentials plan**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
