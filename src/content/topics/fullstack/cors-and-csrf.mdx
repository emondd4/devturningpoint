<Callout type="warning" title="CORS is not auth">
  CORS is enforced by browsers. Non-browser clients are unaffected. Never treat CORS as your authorization layer.
</Callout>

## CSRF

If you use cookie-based sessions, attackers can trick browsers into sending those cookies on cross-site requests. Prefer anti-CSRF tokens or designs that avoid simple cookie session traps for state-changing requests.
