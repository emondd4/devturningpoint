## Why cross-stack observability

A slow page may be React, network, Nest, or SQL. You need correlation IDs and traces that cross boundaries.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Propagate trace context from Next to Nest. Structured logs with request IDs. RED/USE metrics on APIs; watch DB and queue health. Alert on user-impacting SLOs.
</Callout>

## Common mistakes

- **Only logging inside Nest**.
- **Metrics without dashboards/alerts**.
- **PII in logs**.

## Interview angle

<Callout type="interview">
  How do you trace a single checkout from button click to DB commit?
</Callout>

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Add a request ID across Next and Nest.
- [ ] Define two SLIs for a critical API.
- [ ] Sketch an OpenTelemetry path.
