## Why this matters

Teams dump entire repos or ticket histories into prompts, then wonder why answers drift. Context engineering decides what the model is allowed to see.

## Levers

- **Selection** — Retrieve or attach only relevant slices.
- **Ordering** — Instructions, then evidence, then task; keep constraints near the end if models drift.
- **Compression** — Summaries with explicit loss notes.
- **Quotas** — Soft/hard token budgets per section (system, tools, RAG, history).
- **Freshness** — Prefer live retrieval over stale pasted docs.

## Practical checks

- Measure tokens before sending.
- Prefer citations to pasting entire PDFs.
- Keep a “must include” vs “nice to include” list.

## Interview angle

How would you keep a long-running agent from overflowing its context window?
