<Callout type="mental-model">
AI is the broad goal; ML learns from data; deep learning uses neural nets; generative AI samples new content (text, images, etc.).
</Callout>

## Distinctions that matter at work

- **Classical ML** — tabular prediction, ranking, anomaly detection with explicit features.
- **Deep learning** — representation learning for vision/speech/text.
- **LLMs / generative AI** — sequence models producing tokens/media; need grounding and verification.
