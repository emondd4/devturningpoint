## Why this matters

Chatbots without retrieval invent company facts. RAG exists to ground answers in documents you control—and to fail when evidence is missing.

## Pipeline

1. **Ingest** — Collect licensed docs; strip junk; keep source IDs.
2. **Chunk** — Size/overlap trade context vs precision; tables/code often need special handling.
3. **Embed** — Vectors approximate meaning; model choice affects recall and cost.
4. **Retrieve** — Top-k + filters (tenant, locale, freshness).
5. **Generate** — Prompt requires citations; refuse when unsupported.
6. **Evaluate** — hit@k and groundedness beat vibe checks.

## Practical checks

- Log retrieved chunk IDs on every answer.
- Prefer local embeddings when learning; cap paid embedding spend.
- Treat retrieved text as untrusted (prompt injection).

## Interview angle

Explain why better chunking can beat a larger generator model for enterprise Q&A.
