## Why this matters

Teams ship broken AI features when they treat chat demos as systems. You need a mental model of tokens, context limits, and failure modes before LangChain, RAG, or agents.

## Mental model

1. **Tokenization** — Text becomes tokens; cost and context limits are usually token-based.
2. **Context window** — Only a finite prompt+history fits; overflow must be truncated or summarized deliberately.
3. **Sampling** — Temperature/top-p change variability; lower variance helps structured tasks.
4. **Training cutoff / missing tools** — Models do not inherently “know” your private repo or live docs unless you retrieve or attach them.
5. **Hallucination** — Confident wrong answers are expected without grounding and verification (see OWASP LLM misinformation/output risks).

## Practical checks

- Estimate prompt size before sending huge files.
- Prefer structured outputs with schemas when integrating with code.
- Never trust a claim that “tests passed” without seeing command output.
- Treat model suggestions for packages/APIs as hypotheses until verified against official docs.

## Interview angle

Be ready to explain: token vs character, why RAG exists, and how temperature affects reproducibility.
