## Why guessing indexes wastes weeks

The planner chooses among scans and join strategies using statistics. `EXPLAIN` shows the plan; `EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)` runs it and reports actual timing—proof over folklore.

## Mental model

<Callout type="mental-model">
  Nodes flow bottom-up: reads feed joins feed sorts. Compare estimated rows vs actual. Large mismatches hint stale stats or correlated columns. Cost is a planner unit—not milliseconds—until ANALYZE.
</Callout>

```sql
EXPLAIN (ANALYZE, BUFFERS)
SELECT o.id, u.email
FROM orders o
JOIN users u ON u.id = o.user_id
WHERE o.created_at >= CURRENT_DATE - INTERVAL '7 days'
ORDER BY o.created_at DESC
LIMIT 50;
```

Look for Seq Scan on large tables with selective filters, Nested Loop explosions, and sorts spilling to disk.

## Common mistakes

- **Optimizing EXPLAIN without ANALYZE** — Estimates lie when stats are wrong.
- **Micro-benchmarking cold cache only** — Note buffers/shared hits.
- **Ignoring LIMIT’s effect** — Plans change with limits.

## Practice checklist

- [ ] Capture ANALYZE for a slow endpoint query.
