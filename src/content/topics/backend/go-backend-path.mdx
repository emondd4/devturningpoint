## When Go may fit

- High concurrency network services
- Simple deployable static binaries
- Teams valuing explicitness and tooling simplicity

## When not to switch blindly

Rewrites have cost. Choose Go for problem fit—not fashion. Node/Nest remains strong for TS full-stack teams.
